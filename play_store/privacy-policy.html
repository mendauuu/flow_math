Flow Math Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short: Flow Math does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

What the app stores

To show your progress, Flow Math saves the following only on your device:

  • your settings (sound, background, session length, reminder times);
  • your math, focus and meditation session history, levels and XP;
  • focus tasks, steps and “parked thoughts” you type in yourself.

This information is never uploaded. The app has no account, no server and no internet access. It contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools, and it does not use the advertising ID.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for the optional reminders and timer alerts you turn on. They are created on your phone; nothing is sent from a server.
  • Run at start-up: so your reminders are set again after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for gentle haptic feedback.

Deleting your data

In Settings → Clear history you can delete all session history. Parked thoughts can be deleted in the Thoughts screen. Uninstalling Flow Math removes everything it stored.

Sharing

No data is shared with anyone, because no data leaves your device.

Children

Flow Math is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
